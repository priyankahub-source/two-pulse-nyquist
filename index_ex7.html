Two pulses, one Nyquist promise.

Generate raised-cosine and root-raised-cosine filters directly from their definitions. Inspect how the roll-off factor reshapes time and frequency.

Raised cosine

With normalized symbol period $T=1$, the pulse is:

hRC(t) = sinc(t) · cos(πβt) / (1 − 4β²t²)

At t = ±1/(2β), use the removable limit: β/2 · sin(π/(2β)).

Root raised cosine

The spectral square root gives the matched-filter pulse:

hRRC(t) = [sin(πt(1−β)) + 4βt cos(πt(1+β))] / [πt(1−4β²t²)]

At t = 0 and t = ±1/(4β), evaluate their analytic limits explicitly.
